Privacy policy

Home Dashboard is a private application used by a single household.

  • When a household member connects their Google account, the app reads and updates that member's Google Calendar events only to display and manage them on the family's own dashboard.
  • Calendar data is stored only on the household's own home server. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page under Security → Third-party access.
